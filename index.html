About Bar Mubiti

Where Kigali comes
to unwind after dark.

Bar Mubiti brings together everything a good evening needs — brochettes straight off the grill, cold drinks, and a lively atmosphere that fills up as the sun goes down. Whether you're stopping by after work or settling in for the night, there's a table with your name on it.

Expect a social, food-focused crowd, attentive service and the kind of easy hospitality that keeps regulars coming back.
